01 / The Problem

Bug reporting was fragmented, repetitive, and expensive in tester time.

QA testers often need to capture footage, reproduce an issue, identify project context, collect supporting media, search for similar existing bugs, write and format a clear report, attach evidence, and submit everything into project management systems.

That work can stretch across multiple tools and repeated manual steps. TestBuddy was shaped around reducing that friction without reducing the quality, consistency, or usefulness of the final bug report.

Screenshot Placeholder TestBuddy interface

02 / The Solution

One platform built around the tester's workflow.

TestBuddy consolidated key QA reporting needs into a purpose-built internal platform, reducing tool-switching and helping testers move from evidence to report with less repeated effort.

03 / System

A tester-focused application connected to shared cloud services and project systems.

At a high level, TestBuddy connected a desktop application experience to cloud-backed data and existing project tools, keeping the day-to-day workflow centered on the tester while supporting broader team operations.

05 / Impact

Designed around measurable workflow improvement.

TestBuddy was piloted with approximately 60 users, with an estimated workflow improvement of roughly 75% in bug-writing effort. The platform was also designed with a much larger tester population in mind, shaping the work around reliability, consistency, and operational scale.
