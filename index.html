About Cyberus Labs

Cyberus Labs is a personal engineering lab focused on transforming ideas into real-world solutions.

It brings together software, automation, product design, and fabrication to explore what can be built when digital systems and physical making share the same workbench.

Danny Bouchard is the creator behind Cyberus Labs, guiding each project with a builder's mindset: understand the problem, design the system, make it real, and keep improving it.
