About Cyberus Labs

Engineering without artificial boundaries.

Cyberus Labs is an independent engineering and product studio focused on turning ideas and real-world problems into working solutions.

The studio combines software engineering, automation, hardware integration, product development, and digital fabrication to build across the line between digital systems and physical outcomes.

Cyberus Labs was founded by software engineer Danny Bouchard, whose background includes software development, QA tooling, test automation, cloud systems, system architecture, and technical product development.
